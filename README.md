# Mandalaye Park — Website Redesign Concept 2

A second, separate website redesign concept for Mandalaye Park Estate, a family-run farm tourism and rural estate business on French Island, Victoria, Australia. Bold cinematic editorial design with a white theme.

## About the business

- 142-acre rural estate on French Island, in Westernport Bay, about 1 hour 30 minutes from Melbourne.
- Family-run farm tourism, accommodation, weddings and special events. Established 2012.
- Contact: Celia Fisher — 0417 392 100 — c.fisher1@live.com — 39 Mandalaye Park Road, French Island VIC 3921.

## What's on the page

- Full-screen hero with stats bar, scrolling marquee, story with photo collage, experiences in alternating image panels
- Four accommodation suites (Luxury Cottage, The Bell Tower Suite, The Manager's Quarters, The Field View) with links to their Airbnb listings
- Weddings, French Island story with timeline-style travel steps, mosaic photo gallery with lightbox, enquiry section

## Notes

- All business content is taken from the existing Mandalaye Park website (mandalayepark.com). No prices, availability, testimonials, or business details have been invented.
- The enquiry form is a demo: it prepares a copyable summary and states plainly that nothing has been sent.
- Photography is a mix: the estate's real photographs (hotlinked from mandalayepark.com) and AI-generated imagery (in `assets/`, used on the client's direction for this second concept).
- This is a separate concept from the first Mandalaye Park redesign; the two are independent projects.

## Run it

Open `index.html` in a browser (the `assets/` folder must sit alongside it), or deploy the repo to GitHub Pages / Vercel.
